      Payslips

      Send a photo of your payslip to Claude in the chat, paste the PAYSLIP line you get back here and tap Add. The page learns your rates from the last 4 payslips. ✓ in Weeks = amount from a payslip.

        Settings

        Break: 45 min is deducted from every shift (as the agency does).

        Rates update by themselves from each payslip you add (you can also type them). Gross hourly rate including holiday pay. No Saturday rate = normal rate; a night shift counts to the day it started.

        Take-home pay is worked out like the payslip of 25/10/2026: hours after the break × rate, minus tax (code 1257L w1m1 – each week on its own) and National Insurance (category A), tax year 2026/27. It is an estimate – the real payslip is what counts.
